📜 AMERICAN POOL WHITEPAPER

1. The Racking Layout: 15 object balls (Solids 1–7, Stripes 9–15, and the 8-Ball dead center) plus the white cue ball.

2. Claiming Your Group: The table starts open after the break. The first player to legally pocket a ball claims that group (Solids or Stripes) for the remainder of the match.

3. Consecutive Shots: If you legally pocket a ball belonging to your assigned group, your turn continues (another shot). If you miss or pocket an illegal target, your turn ends.

4. Fouls & Scratches: Pocketing the white cue ball or failing to hit your own group first results in a foul, granting your opponent "ball-in-hand" placement anywhere on the felt.

5. The 8-Ball Finale: The black 8-ball is the final objective. You can only pocket it after completely clearing all 7 of your group balls. Sinking it early or committing a foul on the 8-ball results in an automatic loss.
